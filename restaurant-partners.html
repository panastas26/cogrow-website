02 · Who's a fit

Ready when these are true.

  • The website add-on is active on the account, so the website builder shows up in the back office.
  • The menu is built and live on the POS.
  • The owner or GM can join a 30-minute kickoff call. They need full back-office admin access to add us as a user.
  • Someone can log in where their domain is registered (GoDaddy, Squarespace, Wix), or they're fine buying a new one through the POS.

Missing one? Refer them anyway and tell Paul which one. We'll sort it out on the kickoff call.

Live in about two weeks.

06 · Questions restaurants ask

Answers you can give on the spot.

Do we have to re-enter our menu?

No. It syncs from the POS, and price changes show up on the site automatically.

Who owns the website?

The restaurant. It's in their POS account and they can edit it any time.

We have several restaurants. One site or several?

Each concept gets its own site. Locations of the same concept share one site with a location picker. Pricing is per location.

We already pay for a website (SpotHopper, BentoBox, Wix).

We rebuild it on their POS's website builder, and they can cancel the old service once the new site is live.

Do we lose our web address?

No. We connect their existing domain. Old pages can be forwarded so Google links still work.

What do we need to send?

Just the intake form. A logo file and a few good photos help, but we pull what we can from their current site and socials.

How do we pay?

CoGrow invoices the restaurant directly. Nothing goes through their POS bill.
