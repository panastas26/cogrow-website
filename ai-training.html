Questions

Does my team need to be technical?

No. If they can write an email, they can do this. We work with the people who are most nervous about it first.

The whole team, or just a few people?

Either. A lot of teams start with two or three people who then show the rest.

In person or remote?

Both work. In person tends to stick better for teams that aren't at a desk all day.

How is this different from a course?

A course teaches the tool. We set it up around your documents and your jobs, then sit with your people while they use it.
