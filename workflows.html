What we put AI on

These are common ones. The first call is where we find yours.

  • 01

    Estimates and quotes

    Someone walks the job and gives the details, and AI drafts the estimate and the write-up. Your team edits a draft instead of starting from a blank page.

  • 02

    Job notes into paperwork

    Photos and a few notes from the field become the invoice, the work order and the record. No retyping at the end of the day.

  • 03

    Fast replies to new leads

    A call or form comes in and the right person hears about it right away. The customer gets a real answer instead of waiting until tomorrow.

  • 04

    One weekly report

    Every Monday you get a plain-English summary: jobs done, money in, and where things got stuck. Nobody has to chase numbers.

  • 05

    Chasing unpaid invoices

    Polite reminders go out on schedule, in your voice, without anyone having to remember.

  • 06

    What your senior people know

    The fixes and shortcuts that live in one person's head get written down somewhere the whole team can ask.

How we build it

It fits your business. Not the other way around.

Your tools

You don't switch to a new platform. We connect what you already pay for, like your email, CRM, accounting and shared drive.

Your voice

Anything that goes to a customer reads like your team wrote it, because it learns from your real emails and documents.

Your call

Nothing runs on autopilot. A person sees the work and approves it before anything goes out.

A recent build

Real estate
Multifamily investor

Deal underwriting, from the broker's PDF

An investor was building every deal model by hand in a spreadsheet. Now they drop in the broker's offering memo. The app reads it, fills in the model against their own return targets, flags pass or pursue, and drafts the memo and a letter of intent.

Someone still reads every deal and makes the call. They just start from a finished draft.
